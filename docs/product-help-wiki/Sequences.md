# Sequences

Sequences help teams run repeatable outreach without manually writing every follow-up.

## When to Use a Sequence

Use sequences for structured communication such as:

- Lead follow-up.
- Trial onboarding.
- Renewal reminders.
- Re-engagement.
- Event follow-up.

Do not use sequences for sensitive, one-off, or high-context conversations.

## Sequence Quality

A good sequence has:

- A clear audience.
- A specific goal.
- Short, human messages.
- Reasonable timing.
- A clear stop condition.

## Templates

Templates should feel useful, not robotic. Personalize when possible and avoid sending outdated assumptions.

## Monitoring

Review sequence performance regularly:

- Are replies increasing?
- Are people opting out?
- Are steps firing at the right time?
- Are contacts still in the right audience?

## Best Practice

Use sequences to remove repetitive work, not judgment. Owners should still review important accounts and high-value contacts manually.
