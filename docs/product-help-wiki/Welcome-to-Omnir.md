# Welcome to Omnir

Omnir is a modern CRM for teams that need sales, service, customer communication, and operational visibility in one place.

The product is organized around a few durable objects:

- Contacts: people you communicate with.
- Accounts: companies or organizations connected to those people.
- Leads: new opportunities that still need qualification.
- Deals: active revenue opportunities.
- Tickets: customer support or service requests.
- Activities: calls, meetings, tasks, and follow-ups.

## How to Think About Omnir

Use Omnir as the shared operating record for customer work. A good record should answer:

- Who are we working with?
- What is happening next?
- Who owns it?
- What has already happened?
- What risk or deadline needs attention?

If a record cannot answer those questions, update it before creating more records.

## Main Work Areas

The sidebar groups the app by workflow:

- Sales Operations: pipeline, contacts, accounts, leads, quotes, sequences, and email.
- Service: tickets and your organization's customer Knowledge Base.
- Insights: reports and dashboards.
- Tools: automations and calendar.
- Admin: users, roles, security, configuration, integrations, and billing.

## Product Help vs Tenant Knowledge Base

This wiki is Omnir product help. It explains how to use Omnir.

The app Knowledge Base is tenant content. It is where your team publishes customer-facing support articles for your own customers.

Keep these separate. Product help should teach Omnir. Tenant KB articles should teach your customers.

## Recommended First Steps

1. Finish onboarding.
2. Add users and confirm their roles.
3. Review company profile, portal, and numbering settings.
4. Import or create a small test set of contacts, accounts, leads, deals, and tickets.
5. Configure email, calendar, and integrations.
6. Build reports and dashboards after the team has real workflow data.
