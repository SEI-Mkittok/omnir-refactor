# Quotes

Quotes help sales teams formalize pricing and scope for a deal or account.

## When to Create a Quote

Create a quote when a customer needs a concrete proposal, price, or package summary.

Connect the quote to the relevant:

- Account
- Contact
- Deal
- Products or line items

## Quote Quality

Before sending or sharing a quote, check:

- Account and contact are correct.
- Currency and totals are correct.
- Line items match the agreed scope.
- Expiration or validity period is clear.
- Internal notes do not appear in customer-facing text.

## Numbering

Admins can configure quote numbering in Settings. Use stable prefixes and starting numbers before launch to avoid confusing customers later.

## Quote Status

Use status to keep quotes understandable:

- Draft: still being prepared.
- Sent: shared with the customer.
- Accepted: approved or signed.
- Rejected or expired: no longer active.

## Best Practice

Do not treat quotes as isolated documents. Keep the related deal updated so pipeline reporting and customer communication stay aligned.
