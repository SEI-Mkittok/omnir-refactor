# Contacts and Accounts

Contacts are people. Accounts are organizations. Most CRM work becomes easier when these records are connected correctly.

## Accounts

Create an account for a company, organization, or buying entity.

Useful account fields include:

- Name
- Website or domain
- Industry
- Size
- Owner
- Related contacts, deals, tickets, and notes

Use accounts to understand the full relationship with a company, not just one person.

## Contacts

Create a contact for each person your team communicates with.

Useful contact fields include:

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Account
- Role or title
- Stage or lifecycle state
- Communication preferences

Keep email addresses clean. Duplicate or outdated emails make inbox, sequences, and search less reliable.

## Relationship Quality

A good contact/account setup lets the team answer:

- Who works at this account?
- Which deals are tied to the account?
- Which tickets has the account submitted?
- Who is the primary stakeholder?
- What happened recently?

## Common Mistakes

- Creating accounts for individual people.
- Creating contacts without emails when email is the primary workflow.
- Leaving contacts unattached when an account relationship is known.
- Using notes for information that should be structured fields.

When in doubt, create the durable record first, then attach notes and activities to it.
