# Omnir Help

Welcome to Omnir product help. This wiki explains how to use Omnir CRM across sales, service, administration, reporting, and customer portal workflows.

Omnir also includes a tenant Knowledge Base inside the app. That tenant KB is for your organization's customer-facing support articles. This product help wiki is different: it explains how Omnir itself works.
