# Leads and Conversion

Leads represent people or companies that may become real opportunities but are not fully qualified yet.

## When to Create a Lead

Create a lead when you have potential interest but do not yet know whether it should become an account, contact, or deal.

Examples:

- Website inquiry
- Event scan
- Referral
- Trial signup
- Imported prospect list

## Qualification

Before converting a lead, confirm:

- The person or company is real.
- There is a clear need or use case.
- The owner is known.
- The next action is clear.
- Required fields are complete.

## Conversion

Lead conversion turns early-stage information into durable CRM records.

Depending on your process, conversion can create or update:

- Contact
- Account
- Deal

Admins can configure field mapping so lead data moves cleanly into the target records.

## After Conversion

Do not abandon the record after conversion. Confirm:

- Deal stage is correct.
- Account and contact links are correct.
- Owner is correct.
- Next activity exists.
- Any important lead notes remain visible.

## Best Practice

Use leads to protect your main CRM from noise. Convert only when the relationship deserves ongoing tracking.
