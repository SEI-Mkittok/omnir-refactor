# Calendar

Calendar features help teams connect meetings and activities to CRM records.

## Calendar Connections

Admins configure Google or Microsoft calendar settings. Users may need to authorize their calendar connection before sync works.

## Activities

Activities represent work such as:

- Calls
- Meetings
- Tasks
- Emails

Attach activities to the right records so the team sees customer history in context.

## Meeting Hygiene

Before a customer meeting:

- Confirm the related contact, account, or deal.
- Review recent notes and tickets.
- Add an agenda or objective.

After the meeting:

- Log the outcome.
- Update the deal or ticket.
- Create the next activity.

## Troubleshooting Calendar Sync

If events do not appear:

- Confirm the user authorized the provider.
- Check integration configuration.
- Check provider permissions.
- Refresh and wait for the next sync window.

Admins should verify redirect URLs and provider credentials if multiple users are affected.
