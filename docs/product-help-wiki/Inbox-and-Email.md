# Inbox and Email

Omnir can bring customer email into the CRM so communication stays connected to records.

## Connected Email

Admins configure email providers in Integrations. Users may need to authorize their account before inbox features work.

Once connected, Omnir can show email threads and relate communication to contacts, accounts, deals, or tickets.

## Working from the Inbox

Use the inbox to:

- Review recent customer conversations.
- Connect emails to CRM records.
- Continue a thread with context.
- Spot unanswered or high-priority communication.

Do not use the inbox as a replacement for record ownership. If an email creates work, create or update the relevant record.

## Sending Email

Before sending, check:

- Recipient is correct.
- Related record is correct.
- Template content is appropriate.
- Internal notes are not included.

## Deliverability

If email fails, check:

- Integration connection status.
- Outgoing server configuration.
- User authorization.
- Provider limits or account restrictions.

Escalate persistent delivery issues to an admin.
