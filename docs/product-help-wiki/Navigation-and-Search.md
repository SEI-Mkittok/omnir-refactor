# Navigation and Search

Omnir is built for repeated daily use. Most workflows should be reachable from the sidebar, global search, or a record detail page.

## Sidebar

Use the sidebar to switch between major work areas.

- Dashboard: workspace overview.
- Pipeline: active deals.
- Contacts and Accounts: relationship records.
- Leads: unqualified or early-stage opportunities.
- Tickets: service work.
- Knowledge Base: your organization's customer-facing support articles.
- Reports and Dashboards: performance views.
- Settings: admin and personal configuration.

The sidebar can be collapsed when you need more working space.

## Global Search

Use search when you know the name, email, company, deal, or ticket subject.

Good search habits:

- Search by the most unique term first.
- Use an email address for a person.
- Use a company domain for an account.
- Use ticket subject keywords for support work.

Search returns CRM records. Product help search is separate under Omnir Help.

## Product Help

Use Omnir Help for product questions such as:

- How do I convert a lead?
- What is the difference between product help and the Knowledge Base?
- How do roles and profiles work?
- Where do I configure webhooks?

Product help is global Omnir documentation. It is not tenant-specific.

## Record Detail Pages

Open a record detail page when you need context, history, relationships, comments, attachments, or next actions.

Before leaving a record, check:

- Owner is correct.
- Status or stage is current.
- Next activity is clear.
- Important context is captured in notes or comments.
