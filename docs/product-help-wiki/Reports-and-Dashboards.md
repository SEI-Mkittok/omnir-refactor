# Reports and Dashboards

Reports and dashboards turn CRM records into operating visibility.

## Reports

Use reports when you need a structured answer:

- How many deals are closing this month?
- Which tickets are overdue?
- Which leads were created this week?
- Which accounts have no recent activity?

Reports are most useful when the underlying records are clean.

## Dashboards

Use dashboards for repeated monitoring:

- Pipeline health.
- Ticket volume.
- SLA risk.
- Activity trends.
- Team performance.

Dashboards should be scannable. Put the most important signals near the top.

## Scheduled Reports

Scheduled reports help managers and teams stay aligned without logging in constantly.

Use scheduled reports for:

- Weekly pipeline review.
- Daily support queue review.
- Monthly performance summaries.

## Best Practice

Do not build dashboards before workflow discipline exists. First make sure owners, stages, statuses, dates, and required fields are consistently maintained.
