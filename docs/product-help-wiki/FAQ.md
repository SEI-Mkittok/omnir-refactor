# FAQ

## Is Omnir product help the same as the Knowledge Base?

No. Product help explains how to use Omnir. The tenant Knowledge Base is where your organization publishes support articles for your own customers.

## Can customers see Omnir product help?

Yes. Product help is public and general. It should not contain tenant secrets or customer-specific instructions.

## Can customers see our internal CRM records?

No. Customers use the portal for limited ticket workflows. Internal CRM records remain in the authenticated app.

## Who can change product help?

Product help is edited in the GitHub Wiki and synced into Omnir. Admins can run sync from Settings > Product Help.

## Why does an article say GitHub Wiki?

The GitHub Wiki is the editorial source of truth. Omnir mirrors published wiki pages for fast in-app access and search.

## Why is my tenant KB article not in product help?

Tenant KB articles are organization-specific. Product help is global Omnir documentation.

## Where should I put an article for customers?

Use the tenant Knowledge Base in Omnir.

## Where should I put an article for internal Omnir users?

Use the product help wiki if it explains how to use Omnir. Use internal docs only if it is operational, engineering, or private company context.
