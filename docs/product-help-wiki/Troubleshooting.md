# Troubleshooting

Use this checklist before escalating an issue.

## Page Will Not Load

Try:

1. Refresh the page.
2. Check your network connection.
3. Sign out and sign back in.
4. Confirm the workspace is available for other users.

If the issue affects everyone, capture the time, route, and any visible error.

## Search Does Not Find a Record

Check:

- Spelling.
- Email address.
- Account domain.
- Whether the record was deleted.
- Whether you have permission to view the record.

## Permission Denied

Ask an admin to review:

- Your role.
- Your profile permissions.
- Sharing rules.
- Group membership.
- Field write controls.

## Email or Calendar Not Syncing

Check:

- Provider connection status.
- Whether authorization expired.
- Admin OAuth configuration.
- Provider account restrictions.

## Product Help Looks Empty

Ask an admin to open Settings > Product Help and run sync. Product help is mirrored from the public GitHub Wiki into Omnir.

## What to Include When Reporting a Bug

- What you were trying to do.
- The page or route.
- Exact error text.
- Time it happened.
- Whether it happens for other users.
- Screenshots if they clarify the issue.
