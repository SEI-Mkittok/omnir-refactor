# Knowledge Base vs Product Help

Omnir has two different help concepts. They should not be mixed.

## Tenant Knowledge Base

The tenant Knowledge Base lives inside your Omnir workspace.

Use it for:

- Customer support articles.
- Product or service FAQs for your customers.
- Ticket deflection.
- Portal-facing help content.

This content belongs to your organization. It can vary by tenant.

## Omnir Product Help

Omnir product help is this wiki and the mirrored in-app product-help pages.

Use it for:

- Explaining Omnir features.
- Teaching admins how to configure the app.
- Helping agents and managers use workflows.
- Answering questions about navigation, settings, permissions, and CRM behavior.

This content is global. It should not contain tenant-specific customer support content.

## Quick Rule

If the article teaches your customers about your business, put it in the tenant Knowledge Base.

If the article teaches your team how to use Omnir, put it in Omnir product help.
