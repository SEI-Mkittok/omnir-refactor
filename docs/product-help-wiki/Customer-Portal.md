# Customer Portal

The customer portal gives external customers a focused place to submit and track tickets.

## What Customers Can Do

Depending on configuration, portal users can:

- Sign in.
- View their tickets.
- Create a new ticket.
- Add comments.
- Track status changes.

The portal is intentionally narrower than the internal app. Customers should see what helps them get support, not internal operational detail.

## Portal Setup

Admins should review:

- Portal enabled state.
- Portal display name.
- Announcement text.
- Default assignee.
- Menu and shortcut configuration.

Test the portal before sending links to customers.

## Customer-Facing Communication

Write ticket comments as if the customer will read them. Use internal notes for internal reasoning, escalation details, or sensitive operational context.

## Security

Portal access is separate from internal staff access. Do not invite customers as internal CRM users unless they are actually part of your organization.

## Best Practice

Keep portal language plain and action-oriented. Customers usually visit the portal because something needs attention.
